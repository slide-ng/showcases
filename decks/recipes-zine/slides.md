# Spain: Tortilla Española

Potatoes, eggs, patience

---
layout: recipe
serves: "4"
time: 45 min
---

::ingredients::

### Ingredients

- 6 eggs
- 4 waxy potatoes
- 1 onion (*cebolla*)
- 250 ml olive oil
- salt

::default::

# Tortilla

1. Slice the potatoes and the onion thin.
2. Fry slowly in the oil until soft, not brown.
3. Drain, mix with the beaten eggs, rest **10 min**.
4. Cook on a low heat, flip with a plate, cook again.

![A tortilla on a plate, one slice pulled out](/tortilla.svg){width=220}

# Austria: Apfelstrudel

Dough you can read a newspaper through

---
layout: two-cols
layoutClass: gap-10
---

# Strudel

- **Dough:** flour, water, oil, a pinch of salt. Rest it for an hour, then stretch it over the backs of your hands until you can read a newspaper through it.
- **Filling:** sour apples, breadcrumbs toasted in butter, sugar, cinnamon, raisins.
- **Bake:** roll it up with the cloth, 40 minutes at 190 °C, then dust with icing sugar.
